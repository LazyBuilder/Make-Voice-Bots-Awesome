Speaker Variation and Accessibility

Covers differences in how people speak. Includes accents, dialects, code-switching, fast speech, slow speech, soft speech, shouting, stuttering, dysarthric speech, older adult speech patterns, children’s voices, and other accessibility-related variation.

Live call: direct impactPost-call processing: possible risk
THE APPROACH

What to address in this area

  • Test recognition, timing, and recovery with the range of speakers your product serves.
  • Make an alternate input or human support route reachable after repeated recognition failures.
WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE

The caller can complete the task or reach a usable alternative without being trapped in repeated failed recognition.
