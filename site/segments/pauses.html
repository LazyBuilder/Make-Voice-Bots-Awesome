SEGMENT 02 / 12 · 8 BEHAVIORS

Hesitation, Pauses, and Floor-Holding

Covers moments where the user pauses but has not finished speaking. Includes filled pauses, silent thinking, lookup pauses, memory search, breath pauses, and chunked reading of numbers or IDs.

Live call: direct impactPost-call processing: possible risk
THE APPROACH

What to address in this area

  • Tune waiting behavior for the task: reading an identifier can need a different pause policy from a short yes/no answer.
  • Separate a temporary partial answer from a complete value; use a bounded, helpful check-in when silence continues.
WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE

The caller can finish the answer and the final complete value is used exactly once.
